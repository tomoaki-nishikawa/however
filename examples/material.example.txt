Acme Bookkeeping — Plans and Pricing (fictional sample material)

Plans
- Starter: up to 50 invoices per month. US$19 per month, billed annually. One user.
- Team: up to 500 invoices per month. US$79 per month, billed annually. Up to 5 users. Extra users US$9 per user per month.
- Enterprise: unlimited invoices. Quote only. Single sign-on and audit logs are Enterprise-only.

Onboarding
- Starter and Team: self-serve, usually live within 1 day.
- Enterprise: guided onboarding, typically 2–4 weeks.
- Migration from another bookkeeping tool: one-time fee of US$300 (Team) or included (Enterprise). Not available on Starter.

Notes
- Prices exclude sales tax.
- Acme does not provide tax advice. Questions about how a specific transaction should be taxed must go to a licensed accountant.
- Annual plans can be cancelled with a prorated refund within the first 30 days only.
